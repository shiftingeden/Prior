Common questions

Why won't it show me any measurements?

Prior refuses to measure when the two photographs are too different for the numbers to mean anything — usually because the lighting changed. It will tell you which check failed. The usual fixes are to shoot in the same place at the same time of day, and to use the ghosted overlay to match your pose.

Imported photographs never unlock measurements, because Prior doesn't know what camera or lighting produced them. Take at least two photos inside the app for the same area.

The second photo sits too high or too low.

This happens with close crops — a chin or lip shot has no eyes in frame for Prior to anchor to — and with imported photos taken at a very different distance. Open the photo from the timeline and tap Adjust alignment. Switch the preview to Difference and nudge until the overlap goes dark; that's the two lining up.

Are my photos private?

They never enter your camera roll and never leave your phone. Prior makes no network requests at all — there is no account and no server. You can add a Face ID lock in Settings, and Prior covers its own screen in the app switcher so a preview of your face isn't cached outside it.

What do the numbers actually mean?

Each is a stated arithmetic quantity measured inside a region and reported as a change from your own earlier photograph — mean a* in CIELAB for redness, ridge response for line structure, spread of lightness for tone evenness, and so on. Tap the metric to see the method written under it.

There is no overall score and no grade, deliberately. A single number summarising a face is not a measurement of anything.

Can Prior tell me if a treatment worked?

It can tell you what changed between two photographs. It cannot tell you why, and it doesn't try. Prior does not diagnose anything and is not a substitute for a clinician's opinion.

I lost my phone. Can I get my photos back?

Only if they were in your device backup — which they are by default, unless you turned on Keep photos out of iCloud backup. Prior holds no copy of anything, so there is nothing we can restore for you.

Reminders aren't arriving.

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Prior is allowed. Reminders are timed from your last photo, not a fixed date, so taking a photo early moves the next one.

Suggestions
