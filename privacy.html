Privacy Policy

Prior — Then & Now · Last updated 26 October 2026

Prior transmits nothing. It has no account, no server, and no networking code of any kind, so nothing you do in it ever reaches us or anyone else. Your photos are stored encrypted on your device — and this page describes exactly what the app computes and stores there, including the face data covered below.

What Prior stores

Everything Prior holds — photographs, dates, treatment notes, costs and settings — lives only in the app's own storage on your device. Photographs are written to the app's Application Support directory with iOS file protection, which means they are encrypted at rest whenever your phone is locked. They are never written to your Photos library.

What Prior sends

Nothing. The app contains no networking layer, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising framework and no third-party software development kits. There is no server to send anything to.

Your photo library

Prior never reads your photo library. To import older photos you pick them in Apple's own photo picker, and Prior receives only the photos you choose. If you export a comparison and choose to save it, Prior adds that one image or video to your photo library with add-only permission. Nothing from your photo library is collected, uploaded or shared, and your Prior photos are never copied into it unless you export them yourself.

The optional unlock purchase

Prior is free to try. If you choose to unlock it, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, using Apple's own purchase screen. Apple tells the app only whether the unlock has been bought. We never see your name, Apple Account, payment details or anything else about you, and none of your photos or notes are involved.

Permissions

  • Camera — to take your photographs. Required.
  • Face ID — only if you turn on the app lock. Prior receives a yes-or-no answer from iOS and never sees biometric data.
  • Photos, add-only — only when you tap Save on an export. Prior never requests permission to read your library; importing older photos uses the system picker, which runs outside the app and hands Prior only the images you explicitly choose.
  • Notifications — only if you set a reminder. Reminder text contains the area name and nothing else.
  • Motion — while the camera is open, Prior reads how far your phone is tilted so it can tell you when you are holding it differently from your baseline photo. The angle is recorded with the photograph; nothing else about your movement is read, stored or transmitted.

On-device analysis

Everything Prior measures is arithmetic performed on your device: two photographs are compared and the differences reported. No photograph is ever sent anywhere to be analysed, because there is nowhere to send it to.

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence you can optionally turn on a short written reading of your measurements. It is generated entirely on your device by Apple's on-device model, and it is given only the numbers Prior has already computed — never your photographs. It cannot be given them: the on-device model reads text, not images. This is off unless you switch it on.

Face data

Prior looks at faces in order to line photographs up. Apple asks every app that touches face data to answer the same questions, so here they are, plainly.

  • What is collected — facial landmark coordinates, such as the positions of your eyes, and facial measurements derived from them. They are computed on your device by Apple's Vision framework.
  • Why — to align photographs of the same area over time, and to guide your pose live in the camera so a new photo matches your baseline.
  • Where it is stored — on your device only, encrypted at rest, alongside the photograph it describes. Like everything else Prior stores, it is excluded from your iCloud backup by default.
  • Who it is shared with — no one. There are no third parties, and the app contains no networking code to share it with.
  • How long it is kept — as long as the photograph. Delete the photo, the area, or the app, and the face data is deleted with it.

Prior does not use TrueDepth or ARKit. It uses only the standard camera and Apple's 2D Vision requests — no depth map or 3D scan of your face is ever captured.

Backups

By default your photographs are excluded from your device backup, which means they exist only on that one phone and no copy reaches iCloud. That is the strongest privacy position, and it is also the one where losing the phone loses the record. If you would rather your photographs survive a lost device, turn off Settings → Privacy → Keep photos out of iCloud backup. The app states the trade-off at the switch.

Deleting your data

Delete a photo, an area, or all photos in an area from inside the app, and the underlying files are removed immediately. Deleting the app removes everything. Because Prior holds no data outside your device, there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Children

Prior is not directed at children and transmits no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, this page will say so before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label will change accordingly.
